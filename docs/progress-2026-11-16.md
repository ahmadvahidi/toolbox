# Progress — what the last week of reels actually changed

Written 2026-11-16 after documenting five reels (blender-skills, Harness², Deep-Live-Cam, OpenMausBot,
CBC Marketplace). This is the "so what": which findings became something we can act on, in priority
order by **money speed**, and exactly where the artifact lives. A finding that is not in this file did
not change anything.

## 1. Ready to use now — highest revenue leverage

**The 40 s tool-promo format, motion edition (built, rendered, verified).**
Source lesson: the `@reporadarai` blender-skills reel (README scroll + TTS). We rebuilt it in the
`motionmaxxing` runtime and shipped a working 42.5 s piece with hard cuts on the VO onsets, per-beat
camera drift, a light sweep at every cut, 12 looping sparks, count-up, typing, chain build.

- Format spec: `workspace/team/formats/tool-promo-40s.md`
- Editable film: `motionmaxxing/film-toolpromo/index.html` (render ≈ 2 min, CPU only)
- Finished: `team/formats/demo-blender-skills/tool-promo-v2-motion.mp4`
- Why it matters: our faceless tool/AI lane needs cheap, credible video. This is ~2 minutes of compute
  per piece with no footage, no actor, no stock. It is the fastest content we can produce at volume.

## 2. Next build — the lane the evidence points at

**"Test it in public" reviews beat tool announcements.** Measured this week on the same platform:
README-scroll promos 78 and 122 likes; a talking-head Fact-or-Hype review **1,891**; a 113-second
CBC data-privacy investigation **505,000 views**. The differentiator is never production value — it is a
**specific test that could fail** plus a verdict.

Concrete next artifact (not yet made): a 60-90 s review of one tool **we can actually run on this box**,
in the Fact-or-Hype shape: claim in 11 s → a public test → two specific complaints → one-word verdict.
Candidates already verified here and therefore safe to test honestly: `newera` (3D home design MCP),
`harness2-demo`, `colibri` (35B in 3 GB), `Edge0` (35B at 6 tok/s). The format notes are in
`downloads/DfaBNAXojMT/NOTES.md` (last section) and `downloads/tt-7707759965955476757/NOTES.md`.

## 3. Adopt — improves our own output quality, no spend

**Harness²'s lesson format, not its benchmarks.** Ran the local demo (`harness2-demo --k 2`, 28
executions, 0 failed). What it produces per improvement is: `cue → lesson → evidence → confidence`, with
the cue written as a **condition** ("when a ledger includes credits and debits") so it can fire later.
Our skills currently record rules and pitfalls but rarely the cue or the measured evidence.

Action: when a skill is patched after a failure, append the lesson in that four-field shape. Start with
the two skills that were corrected this week (`google-workspace` troubleshooting row, `social-media-download`
learn-from-links note). No new dependency, no model change.
